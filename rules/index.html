THE RULEBOOK

Follow these guidelines to keep VAJRA SMP fair and enjoyable.

01. GENERAL RULES

  1. Respect all players and staff members.
  2. Harassment, bullying and hate speech are not allowed.
  3. Do not use hacks, cheats, exploits or unfair modifications.
  4. Do not spam messages or advertisements in chat.
  5. Impersonating staff members is prohibited.
  6. Do not exploit server bugs. Report them to staff.
  7. Follow staff instructions and maintain a friendly environment.

02. SURVIVAL RULES

  1. Do not grief or destroy another player's builds.
  2. Do not steal items from another player's storage.
  3. Do not claim land that belongs to another player.
  4. Do not use farms or machines to intentionally lag the server.
  5. Keep your builds and farms within server performance limits.
  6. Do not exploit duplication glitches.
  7. Respect other players' trading agreements.

03. LIFESTEAL RULES

  1. Do not use hacked clients or unfair advantages.
  2. Do not exploit glitches to gain hearts or items.
  3. Do not use alternate accounts to abuse gameplay systems.
  4. Do not intentionally crash or lag the server during fights.
  5. Do not scam players in staff-mediated trades.
  6. Respect server-specific heart and combat mechanics.

04. PVP RULES

  1. No hacked clients, kill aura or unfair combat modifications.
  2. No combat exploits or abuse of unintended mechanics.
  3. Do not use bugs to gain an unfair advantage in fights.
  4. Do not intentionally lag or crash the PvP area.
  5. Respect other players and keep PvP competitive.
  6. Follow any additional arena-specific restrictions.

PUNISHMENT SYSTEM

Rule violations may result in the following actions depending on severity.

01 Warning
02 Kick
03 Temporary Ban
04 Permanent Ban
Note: Staff may choose a suitable punishment based on the seriousness and frequency of the violation. Severe violations may result in an immediate ban.
